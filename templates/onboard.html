Jade troubleshooting
  • Use a Chromium-based desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave) with Web Serial support.
  • Close any other tab or desktop app (Green, Sparrow, etc.) connected to the Jade.
  • The first unlock contacts Blockstream's pinserver — ensure you have a working internet connection.
  • Confirm the PIN on the device when prompted.
